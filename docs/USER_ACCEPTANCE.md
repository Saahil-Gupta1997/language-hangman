# User acceptance — five learner sessions (gate G10)

**Status: no learner sessions have been run.** The records below are empty. They must be
filled in by the person who runs each session, from what actually happened. Never fill
them from automated tests or AI-generated observations. With five people, results describe
this candidate's usability and can surface problems; they are **not** evidence that the app
improves learning.

## Participants

Five people who read short, simple English (about CEFR A2) and have not used Language Hangman
before. The owner still has to say who the app is for (DEC-15), which may change this. Record
only a code (P1–P5), an age band and a self-rated English level. Do not record names in this
repository.

Before the session, explain in plain language: what the session is for, that it is the app
being tested (not them), that they can stop at any time, what will be written down and where
it is kept. Record consent (yes/no and date) outside this repository.

## Setup

- A Windows computer with the rc6 installer (check its SHA256 matches ACCEPTANCE.md) or the
  app already installed. Record the OS, display scaling and whether Python is installed.
- Sessions use the English Beginner list unless the owner decides otherwise. These words have
  not been reviewed, and the feedback lines are sarcastic on purpose: tell participants so.
- A fresh profile for each participant; delete it afterwards (Settings › Delete my profile).
- The facilitator observes and only helps when the participant is stuck for about a minute
  or asks. Every intervention is recorded.

## Tasks (read aloud; do not demonstrate)

1. "Create your own profile." (Start the timer when the sign-in screen appears.)
2. "Start practising English at Beginner level, and play until you finish one word."
   (Stop the timer when the first round result appears: time to first completed round.)
3. "Play two more words, then end your session."
4. "Start again. Close the program in the middle of a word. Open it again and carry on."
5. "End that session, then try Review my words." (It replays the words they finished: does
   that make sense to them?)
6. "Find out how you did." (Reports.)
7. Next day (optional, by message or call): "What do these words mean?" for five words they
   met. Do not show the app. Record answers verbatim.

## What to record per session

| Measure | How |
| --- | --- |
| Time to first completed round | Stopwatch from sign-in screen to first round result |
| Assistance required | Count and describe each prompt or help given |
| Session completion | Did they play three words and end the session without help? |
| Confusing steps | Where they hesitated, misread or asked; their words, verbatim where possible |
| Progress recovery | After closing mid-word, was the same word with the same guesses there? |
| Next-day meaning recall | Number of the five meanings given correctly (exploratory only) |
| Defects | Steps to reproduce, expected and actual result; add to DELIVERY.md |
